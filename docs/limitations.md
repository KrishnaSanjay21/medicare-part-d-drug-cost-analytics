# Limitations

- The source is aggregated public-use data, not patient-level claims.
- The dashboard cannot measure adherence, diagnoses, health outcomes, plan liability, rebates, or causal effects.
- Beneficiary counts can be suppressed for privacy and are not additive across drugs.
- Total drug cost includes multiple payment sources and is not Medicare-only spending.
- Geographic comparisons are descriptive and are not adjusted for population size, case mix, benefit design, or local prices.
- Flags identify CMS drug categories; they do not indicate inappropriate prescribing.
